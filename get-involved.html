🏰 Get Involved in Vithuanum

The Principality of Vithuanum is more than its government and history. It is a community built by its citizens and visitors.

There are many ways to get involved, whether you are interested in creativity, politics, diplomacy, history, media, or simply taking part in national events.

👤 Become a Citizen

Visitors can apply to become citizens of Vithuanum. Citizenship allows you to become an official member of the Principality and participate in national activities.

  • Complete the citizenship application
  • Agree to respect the Constitution and laws
  • Take the Oath of Citizenship
  • Become an active member of the community

Ready to become a citizen?

🎨 Culture & Creative Projects

Help build the culture, history, and identity of Vithuanum through creative projects.

  • 🎨 Create Vithuanian artwork
  • 🗿 Design monuments
  • 🎵 Create a national anthem
  • 📸 Share photographs
  • 📰 Write newspaper articles
  • 📚 Contribute to the national archives
  • 💻 Help develop the official website
  • 🎬 Make TikToks and videos about Vithuanum
  • 🧑‍🎨 Create posters and fictional historical propaganda-style artwork

🏳️ Flag Design Contests

Show your creativity by taking part in Vithuanum flag design contests. Create new designs, regional flags, or ideas for future national symbols.

🎨 National Art Competitions

Artists and creators can participate in national art competitions and showcase their work to the Vithuanum community.

Artwork can help develop the culture, identity, and creative spirit of the Principality.

🏛️ Political Debates

Citizens can take part in political debates and discussions about the future of Vithuanum.

Debates can cover government, laws, national projects, diplomacy, the economy, and other topics affecting the Principality.

All debates should remain respectful and follow the Constitution and community rules.

🌍 Micronation Conferences

Vithuanum may participate in conferences and events involving other micronations.

These events provide opportunities to meet other micronations, exchange ideas, discuss diplomacy, and represent Vithuanum internationally.

🏆 National Events

Vithuanum may organise special events, competitions, and community activities throughout the year.

These could include:

  • 🏳️ Flag design contests
  • 🎨 National art competitions
  • 🏛️ Political debates
  • 🌍 Micronation conferences
  • 🎨 Creative challenges
  • 📸 Photography competitions
  • 📰 Newspaper competitions
  • 🎬 Video and TikTok competitions
  • 🏰 National celebrations

💡 Suggest an Idea

Have an idea for a new Vithuanum event, competition, project, or activity?

Citizens are encouraged to suggest ideas that could improve the community and make Vithuanum more active and exciting.
