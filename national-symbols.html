NATIONAL IDENTITY

National Symbols

The national symbols of the Principality of Vithuanum represent its identity, history, values and traditions.

National Flag

The national flag is one of the principal symbols of the Principality of Vithuanum. Its colours and heraldic imagery represent the identity and values of the nation.

Meaning of the Flag

White

White represents peace, unity and openness, reflecting Vithuanum's commitment to peaceful relations and a united national community.

Gold

Gold represents Vithuanum's heritage, sovereignty and national pride, reflecting the historic and constitutional character of the Principality.

Blue

Blue represents stability, loyalty and continuity, while also reflecting Vithuanum's connection to the seas surrounding the United Kingdom.

The Lions

The lions represent strength, courage and sovereignty, symbolising the determination of Vithuanum and its people.

Coat of Arms

The Coat of Arms of the Principality of Vithuanum serves as an official emblem of the nation and is used to represent the Government and the Principality.

Meaning of the Coat of Arms

The Crown

The crown represents the Principality's constitutional monarchy and the authority of the Sovereign.

The Lions

The lions represent courage, strength and the protection of the Principality.

National Anthem

No national anthem has been adopted

The Principality of Vithuanum has not yet officially adopted a national anthem.
