NATIONAL IDENTITY

National Symbols

The national symbols of the Principality of Vithuanum represent its identity, history, values and traditions.

National Flag

The flag of the Principality of Vithuanum is one of the principal symbols of the nation and represents Vithuanum both within the Principality and abroad.

Coat of Arms

The Coat of Arms of the Principality of Vithuanum serves as an official emblem of the nation and is used to represent the Government and the Principality.

National Anthem

No national anthem has been adopted

The Principality of Vithuanum has not yet officially adopted a national anthem.

National Values

The national values of Vithuanum reflect the principles that guide the nation and its community.

Tradition
Peace
Unity
Justice
Creativity
Diplomacy
Honour

Constitutional Recognition

The national identity and symbols of Vithuanum form part of the constitutional framework of the Principality.
