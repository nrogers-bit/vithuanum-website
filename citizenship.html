Citizenship

Become a citizen of the Principality of Vithuanum and take part in our national community.

Become a Citizen

Citizenship connects you to the community of Vithuanum and allows you to participate in the nation's activities, projects and institutions.

Individuals who wish to become citizens must complete the official citizenship application and agree to respect the Constitution and laws of Vithuanum.

Apply for Citizenship →

How Citizenship Works

1. Join the Community

Individuals joining the official community of Vithuanum are initially recognised as Visitors.

2. Complete the Application

Visitors are expected to complete the citizenship application process within three (3) days of joining the server.

3. Application Review

Applications are reviewed according to the requirements of the Principality of Vithuanum.

4. Citizenship

Successful applicants may be granted citizenship and become recognised members of the Vithuanian community.

Visitor Status

New members of the official Vithuanum community are initially recognised as Visitors.

Visitors are expected to complete their citizenship application within three (3) days of joining.

Visitors who do not obtain citizenship within this period may be removed from the official Discord server at the discretion of the Government or authorised moderators.

Individuals removed under this process may rejoin the server and submit a new citizenship application at a later date.

Extensions and Exemptions

The Government may grant extensions or exemptions in exceptional circumstances where a Visitor has demonstrated a genuine intention to become a citizen.

Responsibilities of Citizens

Citizens of Vithuanum are expected to:

  • Respect other members.
  • Follow national laws and server rules.
  • Support the peaceful development of Vithuanum.
  • Represent the nation positively.

Rights of Citizens

Citizens of Vithuanum have the right to:

  • Express opinions respectfully.
  • Participate in national activities.
  • Share ideas for improving the nation.
  • Access national information.
  • Be treated fairly and with respect.
  • Participate in cultural and creative projects.
  • Participate in parliamentary elections, subject to electoral laws and requirements.
  • Stand for election to the National Assembly, subject to electoral laws and requirements.

Removal of Citizenship

Citizenship may be removed due to serious violations of national laws or harmful actions against the community.

Oath of Citizenship

Citizens may affirm their commitment to Vithuanum through the national Oath of Citizenship:

“I pledge to respect the Principality of Vithuanum, uphold its Constitution, support its citizens, and contribute positively to the future of our nation.”

Constitutional Basis

Citizenship and Visitor status are established under Articles V, VI and XIII of the Constitution of the Principality of Vithuanum.
