HISTORY OF VITHUANUM

The History of Vithuanum

Vithuanum has developed through several historical eras since its foundation in 2023. Explore the major periods that shaped the Principality.

2023
ERA I

Cargo

Founding State

Cargo represents the earliest known period in the history of Vithuanum and the origins of the nation.

During this period, Cargo was established as a communist state and served as the foundation for the later development of Vithuanum.

Era II
ERA II

The Kingdom of Rütsi

Monarchy

Following the Cargo period, the nation developed into the Kingdom of Rütsi.

This era introduced a monarchical system and marked an important stage in the political development of the nation.

Era III
ERA III

The Kingdom of Hackendurry

Monarchy

The Kingdom of Hackendurry followed the Kingdom of Rütsi and continued the nation's development under a monarchy.

This period helped establish the political foundations that would eventually lead to the creation of modern Vithuanum.

Present
CURRENT ERA

The Principality of Vithuanum

Centralist Constitutional Monarchy

The modern Principality of Vithuanum represents the current era of the nation.

Vithuanum is governed as a centralist constitutional monarchy, established under the Constitution and supported by a structured government and national institutions.

The Principality is organised into six counties and maintains a growing national community alongside diplomatic relations with other micronations.

The Modern Era

Modern Vithuanum is built around constitutional government, national identity, community and continued development.

  • Centralist constitutional monarchy
  • Six recognised counties
  • Constitution and legal framework
  • Prime Minister and Government
  • National Assembly
  • Diplomatic relations
  • Growing national community

Legacy

Each historical period contributed to the development of the modern Principality. The transition from Cargo, through the Kingdoms of Rütsi and Hackendurry, ultimately led to the establishment of the present Vithuanian state.

The Future

Vithuanum continues to develop through national projects, government institutions, diplomacy, community activities and the participation of its citizens.
