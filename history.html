Our History

The Consitutional History of Vithuanum

Cargo (2023)

The origins of the Principality of Vithuanum date back to **2023**, when the nation was first established under the name **Cargo**. Cargo was organised as a **communist state**, founded on principles of collective governance and equality. During this period, the first foundations of the micronation were laid, including the development of its identity and community. Although the government differed greatly from today, Cargo remains recognised as the beginning of the nation's history. ---

The Kingdom of Rütsi

As the nation developed, a new direction was taken and the state was reorganised under the name **Rütsi**. With this transformation came a constitutional change from a communist state to a **monarchy**. The Crown became the centre of government, and greater emphasis was placed on national traditions, symbolism, and state-building. This marked the nation's first experience under monarchical rule. ---

The Kingdom of Hackendurry

Following further reforms, the nation adopted the name **Hackendurry**. Hackendurry continued as a **monarchy**, building upon the institutions established during the Rütsi era. During this period, the nation's administration, identity, and long-term vision continued to develop. Many traditions that continue today have their roots in this era. ---

The Principality of Vithuanum

After a period of reflection and national reform, the nation adopted its present name, the **Principality of Vithuanum**. Vithuanum established itself as a **centralist state**, with authority centred in the national government while maintaining a constitutional framework and a Head of State. The modern era has focused on: * Establishing a Constitution and legal code * Creating the six counties of Vithuanum * Appointing a Prime Minister * Expanding diplomatic relations with other micronations * Building a welcoming community for citizens The Government continues to promote unity, creativity, diplomacy, and responsible governance while preserving the history of the earlier states that led to the creation of Vithuanum. --- Legacy The Principality of Vithuanum recognises **Cargo**, **Rütsi**, and **Hackendurry** as predecessor states in its historical development. Each era contributed to the nation's evolution, shaping its institutions, traditions, and identity. Together, they form a continuous historical timeline beginning in **2023** and continuing into the present day. AVE VITHUANUM
