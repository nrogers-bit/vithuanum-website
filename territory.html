🗺️ Territorial Organisation

The territory of the Principality of Vithuanum is divided into counties and regions. Each county represents a unique part of the nation's identity, culture, and development.

All counties remain united under the authority of the Sovereign, the Constitution, and the Government of Vithuanum.
