County Administration

Counties may have their own flags, symbols, histories and cultural identities.

While counties may develop their own traditions and identities, they remain an important part of the united Principality of Vithuanum.

Future Expansion

As Vithuanum continues to develop, additional counties and regions may be established according to the Constitution and laws of the Principality.
