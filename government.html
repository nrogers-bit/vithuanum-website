👑 The Sovereign

Sovereign of the Principality of Vithuanum

Noah Rogers

The Sovereign is the Head of State and highest authority of the Principality of Vithuanum.

The responsibilities of the Sovereign include:

  • Protecting the Constitution of Vithuanum
  • Representing the nation domestically and internationally
  • Appointing government officials
  • Approving national decisions
  • Protecting the rights of citizens
  • Guiding the future development of Vithuanum

The Sovereign serves as a symbol of unity, stability, and national identity.

🏛️ Government Structure

The Principality of Vithuanum is a centralist constitutional monarchy.

Authority is centred in the national government while operating under the Constitution and laws of Vithuanum.

The Government assists the Sovereign in managing national affairs, developing policies, and supporting citizens.

🗺️ Local Administration

The territory of Vithuanum is divided into counties and regions.

The six recognised counties are:

  • Eagleshampton
  • Ciocanshire
  • Hurent
  • South Coleena
  • North Coleena
  • Kuross

Each county contributes to the culture and identity of Vithuanum while remaining united under the Principality.

📜 Future Institutions

As Vithuanum continues to grow, future institutions may be established, including:

  • A Parliament

The creation of new institutions will follow the Constitution of Vithuanum.
