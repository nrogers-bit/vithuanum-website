GOVERNMENT OF VITHUANUM

Government

Information about the constitutional structure, institutions and administration of the Principality of Vithuanum.

Constitutional Monarchy

The Principality of Vithuanum is a centralist constitutional monarchy. The Constitution establishes the structure of the Principality and defines the responsibilities and authority of its institutions.

The Sovereign serves as the Head of State, while the Government is responsible for the administration and development of the Principality.

The Sovereign

The Sovereign is the Head of State of the Principality of Vithuanum and serves as the highest constitutional authority within the Principality.

Noah Rogers

Sovereign of Vithuanum

The Sovereign represents the unity and continuity of Vithuanum and exercises the powers assigned by the Constitution and laws of the Principality.

The Prime Minister

The Prime Minister is the Head of Government and is responsible for overseeing the Government and coordinating national administration.

Allu

Prime Minister of Vithuanum

The Prime Minister works with the Sovereign and Government to manage national affairs, develop policy and support the administration of the Principality.

The National Assembly

The National Assembly of Vithuanum is the legislative institution of the Principality.

Members of the National Assembly participate in debates, consider legislation and represent the interests of citizens within the political system of Vithuanum.

The powers, responsibilities and procedures of the National Assembly are established by the Constitution and laws of the Principality.

Government Responsibilities

The Government works to maintain the administration, development and organisation of the Principality.

  • National administration
  • Implementation of government policy
  • International diplomacy
  • Protection of national interests
  • Development of Vithuanum's counties and territories
  • Promotion of national culture and identity
  • Support for citizens and community projects
  • Development of national institutions
