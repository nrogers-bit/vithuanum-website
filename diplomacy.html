🤝 Diplomatic Mission of Vithuanum

The Principality of Vithuanum seeks peaceful and respectful relations with other micronations, communities, and organisations.

Diplomacy is an important part of Vithuanum's identity, allowing the nation to build friendships, exchange ideas, and cooperate with others.

🏛️ Diplomatic Principles

The diplomatic policy of Vithuanum is guided by:

  • Peaceful cooperation
  • Respect between nations
  • Friendship and communication
  • Cultural exchange
  • Mutual support

🌍 International Relations

The Government of Vithuanum may establish:

  • Alliances
  • Friendships
  • Diplomatic agreements
  • Embassies
  • Partnerships with other micronations

🏰 Diplomatic Representatives

Representatives of Vithuanum act on behalf of the Principality and must represent the nation with respect, responsibility, and honour.

The Minister of Foreign Affairs, when appointed, shall oversee diplomatic relations and international communication.

📜 Current Diplomatic Relations

Vithuanum continues to develop relationships with other micronations and communities.

Official alliances, partnerships, and diplomatic agreements will be recorded here as they are established.

  • No official alliances currently recorded
  • Diplomatic expansion ongoing

🕊️ Diplomatic Goals

The Principality of Vithuanum aims to:

  • Create friendships with peaceful micronations
  • Share culture and ideas
  • Develop international cooperation
  • Represent Vithuanum positively
