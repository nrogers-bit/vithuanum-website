FOUNDATIONAL DOCUMENT

Constitution of the Principality of Vithuanum

The supreme law establishing the principles, institutions, rights and responsibilities of the Principality of Vithuanum.

Preamble

We, the founders and citizens of the Principality of Vithuanum, establish this Constitution to create a peaceful, creative, and united micronation. This Constitution shall protect the rights of citizens, guide the government, preserve national identity, and provide a foundation for the future growth of Vithuanum.

Article I: The State

Section 1

The official name of the nation shall be the Principality of Vithuanum.

Section 2

Vithuanum is a micronation dedicated to:

  • Peaceful diplomacy
  • Creativity and culture
  • Community and friendship
  • Respect and cooperation

Section 3

The national symbols of Vithuanum shall include:

  • The national flag
  • The coat of arms
  • The national anthem
  • Other symbols established by the Sovereign

Article II: The Sovereign

Section 1

The Sovereign shall be the Head of State and the highest authority of the Principality of Vithuanum.

Section 2

The duties of the Sovereign include:

  • Protecting the Constitution.
  • Representing Vithuanum.
  • Appointing government officials.
  • Approving national decisions.
  • Protecting the rights of citizens.
  • Guiding the future development of the nation.

Section 3

The Sovereign shall act responsibly and fairly, respecting the values of Vithuanum.

Article III: The Government

Section 1

The Government shall assist the Sovereign in managing the affairs of Vithuanum.

Section 2

Government positions may include:

  • Prime Minister
  • Minister of Foreign Affairs
  • Minister of Culture
  • Minister of Finance
  • Minister of Infrastructure
  • Other positions created when needed

Section 3

Government officials shall serve the interests of Vithuanum and its citizens.

Article IV: The National Assembly

Section 1

The National Assembly of Vithuanum shall be the official legislative body of the Principality of Vithuanum.

Section 2

The National Assembly shall initially consist of five (5) Members of Parliament, hereafter referred to as MPs.

Section 3

MPs shall represent the citizens of Vithuanum as a whole and shall not be assigned to represent individual counties, regions, or territorial divisions.

Section 4

Members of Parliament shall have the right to:

  • Propose legislation.
  • Debate proposed laws and national matters.
  • Vote on legislation.
  • Represent the interests and views of citizens.
  • Question the Government and its ministers on matters concerning their duties.

Section 5

MPs may stand for election as members of a recognised political party or as independent candidates.

Section 6

The National Assembly shall elect or appoint a Speaker of the National Assembly, who shall be responsible for organising Assembly business, maintaining order during debates, and overseeing official votes.

Section 7

The method, timing, and procedures of parliamentary elections may be established by law or by regulations approved by the Sovereign, provided they remain consistent with this Constitution.

Section 8

A proposed law shall require a majority vote of participating MPs to pass through the National Assembly, unless another constitutional requirement applies.

Section 9

Legislation passed by the National Assembly shall be presented to the Sovereign for approval before becoming law.

Article V: Citizenship

Section 1

Citizenship may be granted to individuals who apply and agree to respect the Constitution and laws of Vithuanum.

Section 2

Citizens shall:

  • Respect other members.
  • Follow national laws and server rules.
  • Support the peaceful development of Vithuanum.
  • Represent the nation positively.

Section 3

Citizenship may be removed due to serious violations of national laws or harmful actions against the community.

Article VI: Rights of Citizens

Every citizen of Vithuanum shall have the right to:

  • Express opinions respectfully.
  • Participate in national activities.
  • Share ideas for improving the nation.
  • Access national information.
  • Be treated fairly and with respect.
  • Participate in cultural and creative projects.
  • Participate in parliamentary elections, subject to electoral laws and requirements.
  • Stand for election to the National Assembly, subject to electoral laws and requirements.

Article VII: National Territories and Regions

Section 1

The territory of Vithuanum may be divided into states, provinces, regions, or districts.

Section 2

Territorial divisions may have their own:

  • Flags
  • Symbols
  • Histories
  • Cultural identities

Section 3

All territories shall remain united under the Principality of Vithuanum.

Article VIII: Diplomacy

Section 1

Vithuanum shall seek peaceful relations with other micronations and communities.

Section 2

The Government may establish:

  • Alliances
  • Friendships
  • Diplomatic agreements
  • Embassies

Section 3

Representatives of Vithuanum shall act respectfully when representing the nation.

Article IX: Laws and Justice

Section 1

The laws of Vithuanum shall be created to protect citizens and maintain order.

Section 2

All citizens and officials shall be subject to the laws of Vithuanum.

Section 3

Punishments shall be fair, reasonable, and focused on maintaining a safe community.

Section 4

New legislation may be proposed by Members of Parliament, the Government, or the Sovereign, unless otherwise established by law.

Article X: National Culture

Section 1

Vithuanum shall encourage:

  • Art
  • History
  • Photography
  • Maps
  • Architecture
  • National traditions

Section 2

Citizens are encouraged to contribute to the culture and identity of the nation.

Article XI: Future Institutions

Section 1

As Vithuanum grows, new institutions may be created to improve national governance and administration.

Section 2

Future institutions may include:

  • Courts
  • Local governments
  • Additional government bodies
  • Electoral institutions
  • Other national organisations

Section 3

The creation of new institutions shall be decided through lawful constitutional changes or legislation consistent with this Constitution.

Article XII: Amendments

Section 1

This Constitution may be changed when necessary to reflect the growth and needs of Vithuanum.

Section 2

Amendments require approval from the Sovereign.

Article XIII: Visitor Verification and Citizenship

Section 1

Individuals joining the official community of the Principality of Vithuanum shall initially be recognised as Visitors.

Section 2

Visitors are expected to complete the citizenship application process within three (3) days of joining the server.

Section 3

Visitors who fail to obtain citizenship within the three-day period may be removed from the official Discord server at the discretion of the Government or authorised moderators.

Section 4

Individuals removed under this Article may rejoin the server and submit a new application for citizenship at a later date, subject to the laws and regulations of the Principality of Vithuanum.

Section 5

The Government may grant extensions or exemptions in exceptional circumstances where a visitor has demonstrated a genuine intention to become a citizen.

This amendment shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.

National Values

The Principality of Vithuanum shall be guided by:

  • Tradition
  • Peace
  • Unity
  • Justice
  • Creativity
  • Diplomacy
  • Honour

Oath of Citizenship

“I pledge to respect the Principality of Vithuanum, uphold its Constitution, support its citizens, and contribute positively to the future of our nation.”

Established as the supreme law of the Principality of Vithuanum.
