Government institutions continue to expand

20 August 2026

The Principality of Vithuanum continues to develop its government institutions as the nation grows.

New government positions and ministries are being established to support the administration of national affairs and provide greater organisation across the Principality.

The development of government institutions forms part of Vithuanum's continued effort to build a structured and effective national government.

Further developments and announcements will be published through the official Government website as they become available.
